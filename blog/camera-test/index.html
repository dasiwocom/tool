在线摄像头测试：检测设备、分辨率与闪光灯

Camera Test Online: Check Devices, Resolutions, and Flash

ToolBox Blog · 2026-09-19

摄像头测试工具能做什么

新买的摄像头验货、网络会议前排查图像问题、确认笔记本摄像头是否被占用——用 摄像头测试工具 都能在浏览器里直接完成。它会：

  • 列出摄像头：识别系统中所有可用的摄像头设备，可单独切换。
  • 实时预览：打开后直接看到画面，立刻判断成像是否正常。
  • 检测分辨率：一键列出当前设备支持的所有分辨率，方便排查“画面模糊”是不是分辨率设置不对。
  • 测试闪光灯：手机前置摄像头通常带补光灯，在这里可以直接测试开启/关闭是否正常。

使用步骤

  1. 选择摄像头，点击“打开摄像头”，浏览器弹出授权时选择允许。
  2. 观察实时预览画面是否清晰、方向是否正确。
  3. 点“检测分辨率”查看支持的分辨率列表。
  4. 若设备带补光灯，点“手电筒”开关测试闪光功能。

常见问题

  • 提示被拒绝：在浏览器地址栏重新允许摄像头权限后重试。
  • 提示被占用：可能是视频会议、聊天软件正占用摄像头，关掉后再试。
  • 找不到设备：确认摄像头已连接并已安装驱动。

画面全程在本地处理，视频不会离开你的设备，也不会被录制或上传。

What the camera test tool does

Inspecting a brand-new webcam, troubleshooting video before a meeting, or confirming whether your laptop camera is busy — the Camera Test tool handles all of it right in the browser:

  • List cameras — detects every usable camera on the system and lets you switch between them.
  • Live preview — see the picture instantly and judge image quality at a glance.
  • Detect resolutions — lists all resolutions the current device supports, so a "blurry video" is easy to diagnose.
  • Test the flashlight — phone front cameras usually include a fill light; switch it on and off here to verify it works.

How to use it

  1. Pick a camera and tap "Start camera", then allow the permission prompt.
  2. Check that the preview is sharp and correctly oriented.
  3. Press "Detect resolutions" to see the supported list.
  4. If the device has a light, toggle the "Flashlight" switch to test it.

Troubleshooting

  • Permission denied — re-enable camera access from the browser's address bar and try again.
  • Camera busy — a video call or chat app is probably holding it; close them and retry.
  • No device found — confirm the camera is connected and its drivers are installed.

The video stream is processed locally and never leaves your device — nothing is recorded or uploaded.
