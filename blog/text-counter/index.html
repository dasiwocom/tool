字数统计：字符、单词、行数、中文字数一键统计

Text Counter: Characters, Words, Lines, and CJK Count

ToolBox Blog · 2026-09-19

为什么需要字数统计

投稿平台按字数限制正文、软文按字数计价、论文要求摘要与正文字数范围、内容平台统计中文字数——写东西之前先知道字数，能少踩很多坑。

这个工具统计什么

打开 字数统计工具，粘贴或输入文本，实时得到：

  • 字符数：含空格与不含空格两种口径。
  • 单词数：英文内容按空格分隔计数。
  • 行数：适用于日志、清单类文本。
  • 字节数：判断与数据库字段长度、内容配额是否匹配。
  • 中文字符数：按中文内容平台的习惯单独统计。

小提示

不同平台“字数”定义不同：有的算中文字符，有的算单词，有的全字符。对照平台的统计口径选择对应数字即可，避免因统计标准不一样导致被拒。

Why word counts matter

Publishing platforms cap body length, sponsored posts are priced by the word, theses require abstract and body word ranges, and content sites count Chinese characters — knowing the count up front avoids a lot of pain.

What the counter measures

Open the text counter, paste or type your content, and watch it update live:

  • Characters — both with and without spaces.
  • Words — space-delimited counting for English content.
  • Lines — handy for logs and checklists.
  • Bytes — to check against database field sizes and quotas.
  • CJK characters — counted the way Chinese content platforms do.

A tip

"Word count" means different things on different platforms: Chinese characters on some, space-delimited words on others, every character on a few. Match the platform's definition and you will not get bounced for counting the wrong way.
