图片翻转旋转：水平翻转、垂直翻转与角度旋转

Image Flip and Rotate: Mirror and Turn Free

ToolBox Blog · 2026-09-19

翻转与旋转的用处

拍的视频截图方向不对、合成素材时镜像翻过来才合适、设计稿要求某个元素水平反转——这类“换个方向”的调整用工具做最快，几秒出结果。

这个工具怎么用

打开 图片翻转旋转工具：

  • 水平翻转：左右镜像。
  • 垂直翻转：上下镜像。
  • 双向镜像：水平和垂直同时翻转。
  • 按角度旋转：输入角度自由旋转，修正拍摄方向。

选择方向后点击按钮，输出结果即可下载。

小提示

条形码、文字类图片翻转后会被镜像（内容随之颠倒），不是 bug；需要“只转方向不动内容”时，优先用旋转而不是翻转。全部处理在浏览器本地完成。

Why flip and rotate

A video frame faces the wrong way, an asset only fits mirrored, or a design requires an element reversed — "same picture, different direction" fixes are fastest done by a tool.

How the tool works

Open the flip and rotate tool:

  • Flip horizontally — mirror left to right.
  • Flip vertically — mirror top to bottom.
  • Flip both — horizontal and vertical at once.
  • Rotate by angle — spin freely by degrees to correct orientation.

Pick a direction, hit the button, and download the result.

A tip

Barcodes and text become mirrored after a flip, which is expected — content genuinely turns around. When you want the orientation corrected without scrambling content, prefer rotation over flipping. Everything runs locally.
