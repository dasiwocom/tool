Why crop images

Screenshots drag in unrelated parts, subjects sit off-center, or a scan has ragged edges — keeping the useful portion and dropping the rest is the most common reason to crop.

How the tool works

Open the image cropper:

  1. Choose an image — upload what you want to process.
  2. Select a region — drag on the preview to mark the area to keep.
  3. Crop and download — hit crop and get an image containing only your selection.

Everyday uses

  • Avatars and graphics — cut a square or the exact ratio you need.
  • Clean edges — trim surrounding whitespace or uneven borders.
  • Focus a subject — crop to the area of interest, then resize to a target width.

Cropping happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
