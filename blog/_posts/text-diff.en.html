Why compare two texts

Reviewing edits, rolling back versions, checking config pairs, or reconciling deliverable documents — all of it comes down to "what actually changed". Scanning by eye is slow and error-prone; highlighting the differences is far more reliable.

How the tool works

Open the text diff tool:

  1. Paste the original text on the left.
  2. Paste the new version on the right.
  3. Click compare, and the tool highlights every addition, removal, and replacement between the two.

Reading the result

New lines, deleted lines, and replaced regions each get their own style, so you can scan a long document and jump to the exact spots that changed. Confirm each difference is intended rather than accidental, then move on. Everything runs locally in your browser.
