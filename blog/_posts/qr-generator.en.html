How to create a QR code

Turning a URL, Wi-Fi settings, business-card details, or plain text into a scannable QR code takes seconds. Open the QR generator and follow three steps:

  1. Enter content — paste a link or any text.
  2. Tune the settings — pick the module size (in pixels) and the error-correction level. Higher correction tolerates damage and partial occlusion, so printed codes still scan.
  3. Generate and download — get a PNG of your code and save it anywhere.

Where it shines

  • Sharing links — turn a long URL into a code for posters, business cards, and displays.
  • Wi-Fi sharing — encode a standard Wi-Fi string and let guests scan to connect.
  • Tracking material — add codes to products or documents that link to details on scan.

A quick tip

Keep the classic light background with dark modules — avoid inverted colors or busy backgrounds. The high-resolution PNG is print-ready; for big formats, just raise the module size.
