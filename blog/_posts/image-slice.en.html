Why slice an image

A 3×3 grid posts look sharper than one giant image that platforms compress; huge maps and long screenshots are easier to browse in chunks; game and design work often needs assets split on a grid — the slicer handles all of it in seconds.

How the tool works

Open the image slicer:

  1. Choose an image — upload the source.
  2. Set rows and columns — decide the grid; the tool divides evenly.
  3. Slice — get identically sized pieces.

Common uses

  • Grid posts — publish a 3×3 on social platforms where single tiles stay crisp.
  • Split long images — cut oversized screenshots into readable sections.
  • Split assets — break a full artboard into reusable sub-resources.

Pieces are delivered ready to use, and everything is processed locally in your browser.
