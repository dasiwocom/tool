What Morse code is

Morse encodes letters and digits as dots, dashes, and pauses — the classic long-distance signaling system. Today it lives on in amateur radio, emergency signaling, and the fun of learning a "secret code".

How the converter works

Open the Morse converter:

  • Text to Morse — type letters or digits and see the dot-dash sequence instantly, with automatic spacing between characters.
  • Morse to text — paste a received sequence and get readable text back.
  • Direction switch — flip between the two at any time.

Learning tips

Start with single letters and the common ones you will hear constantly (E, T, A, O), then move to full words. The converter is a great study buddy: translate a signal you could not parse, then re-listen to train your ear.
