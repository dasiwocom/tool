Where circles show up

Profile pictures, contact badges, circular logos, member lists — the circle is the most universal shape for these. Cut any square photo into a circle, and the transparent-background PNG sits cleanly on any theme.

How the tool works

Open the circle crop tool:

  1. Choose an image — upload what you want to turn round.
  2. Set the output size — specify the diameter in pixels.
  3. Pick a fit mode — contain (keeps proportions, may leave margins) or cover (fills the circle, may trim edges).
  4. Generate and download — get a circular PNG with a transparent background.

A tip

Contain feels most natural for avatars; cover fills the circle completely. The output PNG carries an alpha channel, so it layers cleanly over any backdrop color.
