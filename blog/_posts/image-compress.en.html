Why compress images

A single phone photo often lands at 3–5 MB — slow to upload, wasteful of storage, and many platforms refuse "oversized images". Compression shrinks files dramatically with little visible change, perfect for web, chat, and shop uploads.

How the tool works

Open the image compressor and drag a picture in:

  1. Drop the image — drag and drop or pick a file.
  2. Tune quality — the slider balances size against sharpness; lower means heavier compression.
  3. Check the numbers — original size, new size, and the saved percentage appear live before you download.

Tips

Photos look great around 0.7–0.8 quality; screenshots and text use PNG or higher quality so edges stay crisp. Everything runs locally in your browser — the original is never uploaded.
