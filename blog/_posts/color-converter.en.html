Why convert between color formats

Colors are written three different ways all the time: CSS leans on hex like #336699, design tools speak RGB, and adjusting brightness or transparency is easiest in HSL. Everyone trips over the conversions.

How the converter works

Open the color converter:

  • Enter any one format — hex, RGB, or HSL; fill in a single field.
  • Convert live — the other two representations appear automatically, no clicks required.
  • Swatch preview — a color chip confirms what you are looking at.

Typical uses

You grab #2E7D32 from a design file and need its rgba() version with transparency, or you want to shift a brand color's hue in HSL to find a near match. Paste your picked color in and get all three flavors at once.
