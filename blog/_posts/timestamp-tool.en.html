What a timestamp is

A Unix timestamp counts seconds since midnight UTC on January 1, 1970 — the format behind most databases and APIs. The trouble is you cannot read 1789791917 as a date at a glance.

Working with the tool

Open the timestamp converter:

  • Timestamp to date — paste any timestamp and get the local time instantly; millisecond timestamps are supported too.
  • Date to timestamp — pick or enter a date and time, and get its second-based timestamp.
  • Current time — grab "right now" in one click and copy it for API parameters or log files.

When you will need it

Confirming an expiry field while debugging an API, reading timestamps inside logs, or translating "midnight tomorrow" into an API-ready value — this tool handles all of it. Both directions copy in one click, no mental math required.
