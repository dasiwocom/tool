When to stitch images

Chat screenshots are messy sent one by one — a single long image shares far better; comparison panels, flow diagrams, and poster fragments all come together by merging images.

How the tool works

Open the image stitcher:

  1. Add images — queue the pictures in order.
  2. Pick the direction — horizontal stacking side by side, or vertical top to bottom.
  3. Set the gap — optionally leave a pixel margin between pictures.
  4. Stitch and download — merge into one long image in a click.

A tip

Direction and ordering decide the result, so decide "left to right" or "top to bottom" before you add files. The combined image keeps each source's proportions — ready for social posts or documents.
