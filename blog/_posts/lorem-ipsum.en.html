Why placeholder text

Designs, prototypes, layout demos, and templates all need filler before real content arrives — it shows the "general look" to clients and teams. Lorem Ipsum is the classic choice.

How the generator works

Open the Lorem generator:

  • Language — classic Latin placeholder by default; switch to Chinese filler text when reviews feel more natural that way.
  • Sentences per paragraph — controls how dense each block reads.
  • Paragraph count — how many blocks to generate.
  • Generate and copy — once the density looks right, copy it into your page or document.

Usage tips

Latin filler keeps the eye on layout; Chinese filler mimics real review pacing. Generate one or two sentences first to gauge density, then batch — that avoids overflow or a page that looks thin.
