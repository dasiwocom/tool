PDF 转 Word：可编辑文字，还是整页图片保真？

Convert PDF to Word: Editable Text vs Pixel-Perfect Pages

ToolBox Blog · 2026-09-19

PDF 转 Word 的两种思路

打开 PDF 转 Word 工具，选好文件后你会看到一个关键选项——转换方式。它决定了这个 PDF 变成什么样的 Word 文档：

  • 可编辑文本：工具读取 PDF 里的文字内容，按行重新组织成真正的段落，字体、标题、编号都会被还原。转换后的 .docx 可以直接增删改字，像普通 Word 文档一样编辑。
  • 整页图片：把每一页渲染成高清图片，再按 A4 尺寸一张张嵌进 Word。版式、字体、颜色与 PDF 完全一致，但内容本身不可修改，适合归档和交付。

什么时候选哪种

需要改内容就选可编辑文本——比如从合同、论文、报销单里抓文字再整理。文本模式下工具会尽量还原段落结构与标题层级，转完的文档比手工誊一遍快得多。

只看版式就选整页图片——设计稿、扫描件、带特殊字体的画册，用图片模式不会有任何排版漂移，Word 里面长什么样，打印出来就是什么样。

为什么可以放心用

  • 全程本地转换：PDF 解析与 .docx 生成都在你的浏览器里完成，文件不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 免费、不限页数：不收费、不登录、不加水印，一次性处理整个文档。
  • 生成的 .docx 标准规范：可以直接用 Word、WPS 或 LibreOffice 打开。

小提示

扫描件（纯图片型 PDF）没有可提取的文字，这时别选可编辑文本——直接切到整页图片模式即可。若是复印件纸质材料，建议先做 OCR 再转换，效果更好。

Two ways to convert a PDF to Word

Open the PDF to Word converter, pick a file, and you will face one key choice — the output style. It decides what kind of Word document you get:

  • Editable text — the tool reads the text inside your PDF and rebuilds it into real paragraphs, restoring alignment, headings, and numbering. The resulting .docx can be edited like any ordinary Word file: add, remove, or rewrite words freely.
  • Page images — every page is rendered as a high-resolution image and embedded into the document at A4 size. Layout, fonts, and colors match the PDF exactly, but the content itself is not editable — ideal for archiving and deliverables.

Which mode should you pick?

Need to change content? Choose editable text — for pulling text out of contracts, theses, and invoices and tidying it up. The tool restores paragraph structure and heading levels, so you end up far faster than retyping by hand.

Care only about appearance? Choose page images — design mockups, scans, and brochures with unusual fonts keep their exact look, with zero layout drift from the source PDF.

Why you can trust it

  • 100% local conversion — parsing and .docx generation happen in your browser; no file is ever uploaded.
  • Free with no page limits — no account, no watermark, whole documents in one pass.
  • Standards-compliant .docx — opens cleanly in Microsoft Word, WPS, and LibreOffice.

One tip

For scanned, image-only PDFs there is no text to extract — skip editable text and switch straight to page-image mode. For paper scans, running an OCR pass before converting gives noticeably better results.
