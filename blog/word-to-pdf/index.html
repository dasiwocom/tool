Word 转 PDF：不装 Office 也能把 .docx 转成 PDF

Convert Word to PDF Without Installing Office

ToolBox Blog · 2026-09-19

怎么在浏览器里把 Word 转成 PDF

打开 Word 转 PDF 工具，选一个 .docx 文件，接下来只有两步：

  1. 出现预览：工具直接在浏览器里解析文件内容，标题、列表、表格、图片都会按原文排版渲染出来。
  2. 点击“另存为 PDF…”：浏览器会弹出打印窗口，把目标打印机选成“另存为 PDF”，就能得到一个排版与预览一致的 PDF 文件。

为什么推荐这种转换方式

  • 不用装 Office：不需要 Word、WPS 或任何桌面软件，有浏览器的设备就能转。
  • 排版原样保留：PDF 由这份真实排版渲染而来，字体、间距、分页都和你屏幕里看到的一致。
  • 生成真正的 PDF：不是把内容重新抄一遍，而是按“打印页面”输出，交给别人、直接打印都可靠。

关于格式与隐私

工具支持标准 .docx 文件。如果你手里是老版 .doc 格式，先用 Office 或 WPS 另存为 .docx 再转换。

转换全程在你的浏览器本地完成，文件内容不会上传到任何服务器，可以放心处理合同、简历这类敏感文档。

How to convert a Word document to PDF in the browser

Open the Word to PDF converter, choose a .docx file, and you are only two steps away:

  1. A preview appears. The tool parses the document right in the browser and renders headings, lists, tables, and images with their original formatting.
  2. Click "Save as PDF…". The browser opens its print dialog — pick "Save as PDF" as the destination printer, and you get a PDF that matches the preview exactly.

Why convert this way

  • No Office required — no Word, WPS, or desktop software; any device with a browser can do it.
  • Layout stays intact — the PDF is rendered from the real layout, so fonts, spacing, and page breaks match what you see on screen.
  • A genuine PDF output — content is printed as pages, not recreated, so the result is dependable when shared or printed.

Format and privacy

The tool works with standard .docx files. For legacy .doc files, save them as .docx in Office or WPS first.

Everything happens locally in your browser — your document is never uploaded, so it is safe to convert sensitive files like contracts or résumés here.
